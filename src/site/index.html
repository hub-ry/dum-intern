01 THE STORY BEHIND DUM

“

“I’m doing almost everything with AI during my internship. What do I do?”

A question from r/cscareerquestions ↗

That question stuck with us. If AI can finish the ticket but you can’t explain the change, who got better?

Dum flips the usual deal. It’s the eager intern—not the expert doing your job for you. It helps with what you’re ready to do, asks about the decisions you’re learning, and lets your own skills set the boundary.

No judgment. No fake mistakes. Just a capable little teammate whose abilities grow as you do.
